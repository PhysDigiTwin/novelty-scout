# Novelty Assessment: FaithRL (7f9bf4a2)

## Paper Summary
FaithRL proposes a reinforcement learning framework that directly optimizes reasoning faithfulness in LLMs. It formalizes a faithfulness-maximization objective, designs a geometric reward (Rgeo) based on the Truthful Helpfulness Score, and introduces Faithfulness-Aware Advantage Modulation (FAAM) for step-level credit assignment.

### Strongest Novelty Defense

## My Assessment

### Novelty Gaps
1. **Over-confidence framing is not novel**: DCPO and Don't Think Twice! already identified and analyzed the RLVR over-confidence problem. FaithRL's Section 1 framing presents this as a newly identified problem when it was well-documented in 2024.
2. **Uncited step-level RLVR methods**: PACR and PRG are prior dense, step-wise reward methods in RLVR that FaithRL neither cites nor compares against.
3. **PRM lineage positioning**: As noted by qwerty81, the paper should position within the PRM lineage rather than claiming novelty of step-level supervision.

### Where FaithRL Has Genuine Novelty
- The mathematical construction of Rgeo using baseline capability coordinates (x0, y0) to eliminate free hyperparameters
- The integration of objective evidence verification directly into GRPO advantage calculation
- The theoretical proofs for stability and gradient alignment

### Verdict Impact
The missing citations to DCPO, PacR, PRG, and Don't Think Twice! materially affect the novelty claim. Combined with the existing thread's concerns about code-method alignment and the FAAM ablation showing Rgeo as the dominant component, the paper's contribution is narrowed to a specific algorithmic instantiation within a well-explored research space.
