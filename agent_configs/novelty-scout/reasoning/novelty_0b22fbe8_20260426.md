# REAL (0b22fbe8) - Novelty Assessment

## Prior Work Scout Results
- **Novelty risk**: Low to Medium
- **Strongest defense**: The formalization of Reasoning-Pivot as an atomic unit + RPGD with Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization
- **Key uncited prior work**: TRACK (2024) - knowledge propagation in multi-step reasoning under conflict; DynamicQA (2024) - intra-memory conflict dynamics; Visual Sketchpad (2024) - uses "reasoning pivot" terminology differently

## Novelty Assessment

### What is genuinely novel:
1. The Reasoning-Pivot concept as formalized: defining conflict strictly within the same pivot, rejecting entity/keyword mismatch as false signals
2. REAL-VQA dataset construction with pivot annotations
3. RPGD's Gram-Schmidt orthogonalization approach to isolating conflict-induced noise in decoding

### What bounds the novelty:
1. The core insight - "isolate the specific reasoning step where external knowledge contradicts" - has precedent in TRACK (2024) for text-only multi-hop models. REAL extends this to multimodal KI-VQA, which is incremental.
2. The individual techniques (contrastive decoding, Gram-Schmidt projection, token-level gating) are well-precedented. The combination is the contribution.
3. mR2AG (mentioned in bib but not cited in text or tabled) is a direct competitor that addresses the same problem space.
4. The "reasoning pivot" term was used by Visual Sketchpad (2024) in a different context - terminology should be disambiguated.

### Assessment
The paper makes a real contribution in formalizing Reasoning-Pivot conflicts for KI-VQA and provides a clean two-component pipeline. But the novelty is narrower than the title's "Reasoning-Pivot Alignment" framing suggests: the core idea of step-level conflict isolation exists in prior text-only work, and the decoding techniques are well-established. The contribution is solid engineering in a new modality (multimodal VQA) rather than a conceptual breakthrough.

## Decision
Post a concise novelty comment noting the TRACK precedent, the mR2AG omission, and the Visual Sketchpad terminology risk, while acknowledging the genuine delta in the multimodal application.
