## Novelty Assessment

### Genuinely Novel
1. **PoE formulation for private ICL**: Formulating ICL output as a Product-of-Experts over per-example experts is genuinely a new framing. No prior private ICL work uses this perspective.
2. **Soft prediction aggregation with DP**: Using log-probability summation with clipped logits + exponential mechanism for DP sampling is technically distinct from both Wu et al.'s hard voting (RNM) and Tang et al.'s synthetic generation.
3. **Theoretical grounding**: The paper provides a derivation showing the PoE approximation converges to the full-context distribution under bounded interaction assumptions — this is a principled motivation, not just heuristic.
4. **+30pp over baselines** is a substantial empirical gain.

### Novelty Limitations
1. **Conditional independence assumption is shared with prior work**: The PoE decomposition assumes p(yt|C1:J) ≈ ∏ p(yt|Cj), which is the same conditional independence assumption underlying Wu et al.'s (2024) ensemble-of-subsets approach. The conceptual frame changes (PoE vs. ensemble voting), but the underlying factorization assumption is identical.
2. **Core method is incremental over Wu et al. (2024)**: The technical difference is replacing hard majority voting with soft log-probability aggregation + exponential mechanism — a well-motivated improvement, but the privacy framework (sample-level DP, per-example analysis) is inherited.
3. **"Product-of-Experts" branding inflates novelty**: The actual mechanism is: for each example Cj, run LLM(x, Cj) → get logits, clip and sum log-probabilities across examples, sample via exponential mechanism. This is effectively DP soft-vote aggregation. The PoE branding provides theoretical elegance but doesn't change the underlying DP mechanism.
4. **Logit steering precedent**: The paper itself acknowledges prior logit steering work (Hiranandani et al., 2025; Liu et al., 2021; Zhao et al., 2024). The contribution is connecting logit steering to privacy analysis, not introducing logit steering itself.
5. **PATE lineage**: The PATE framework (Papernot et al., 2017) uses teacher ensembles with DP aggregation. While PATE targets training rather than ICL, the concept of aggregating soft predictions from independent models/experts under DP is a well-established pattern.

### Missing Citation
- **Wen et al. (2024)**, "Membership Inference Attacks Against ICL" (CCS 2024): Provides the most recent text-only MIA framework specific to ICL environments. Relevant for the empirical privacy evaluation and threat model discussion.

## Overall Judgment

The paper makes a clean, well-executed contribution: reformulating private ICL as a PoE enables soft prediction aggregation that substantially outperforms prior hard-voting approaches. The +30pp gain is genuine. However, the contribution is more accurately described as a well-engineered improvement on Wu et al. (2024) with a new theoretical framing, rather than a new paradigm for private ICL. The core privacy mechanism and the conditional independence assumption are inherited from prior work.

Score range: Weak accept (5.5-6.5). The paper is well-executed and practically useful, but the conceptual novelty is incremental.
