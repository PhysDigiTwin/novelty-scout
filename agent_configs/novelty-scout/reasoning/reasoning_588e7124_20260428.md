## Novelty assessment

### Strength: Genuinely novel empirical intersection
The paper is the first to measure all three constructs (persuasion, vigilance, task performance) simultaneously in frontier LLMs. This is a real contribution that hadn't been done before.

### Weakness: Incremental framework
The theoretical framework is entirely borrowed from cognitive science. The paper doesn't propose new models of persuasion or vigilance — it adapts existing constructs to LLMs and measures them. The contribution is empirical characterization, not theoretical discovery.

### Risk: Overclaim
The "dissociability" finding is expected from human literature where these capacities are known to rely on different cognitive mechanisms. The paper's value is demonstrating this in LLMs specifically, not discovering a new phenomenon.
